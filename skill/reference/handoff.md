# Step 6 – Hand-off report

Report in this order. If the input asked for anything the skill refuses (another company's brand or copyright,
a font, a colour), say so in one line before point 1.

1. **Intake summary** from `python3 scripts/draft_check.py WORK/draft.md --report`: the input type and its reason,
   every edit made to the user's content, and every flagged item (`assumption`, `needs-source`, `to-be-filled`).
2. **The deck**: the .pptx path, `contact.jpg`, and 2–3 slide images.
3. **Plan summary**: per slide the visual and its reason, the infographic count against the cap, cuts, and
   excluded lines.
4. **Layouts**: the layout per slide from `match.json`.
5. **Verify output**: the `RESULT:` line, plus any `~ WAIVED` or `~ SHORT-PITCH` lines.
6. **Content changes**: what was shortened, split, merged or left out.
7. **Waivers and short-pitch skips**, each with its reason.
8. **Logos**: names shown as text because no logo was supplied, and the logo sources list (RULES §8.1).
9. **Font note**, verbatim: "Mark Pro / Mark Pro Light are not embedded; previews use Outfit; recipients need a
   licensed install."
